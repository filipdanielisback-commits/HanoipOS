# Contributing to HanoipOS

Thanks for helping with research into a possible Darwin/XNU port for the Motorola Moto G60.

## Useful contributions

- Hardware identification with reliable public sources.
- Device-tree, kernel, and boot architecture research.
- Upstream license and provenance reviews.
- Reproducible build or test reports.
- Corrections to documentation.
- Clearly scoped feasibility investigations.

## Before opening a pull request

1. Explain the problem or research question.
2. Include source links and exact revisions where relevant.
3. Identify the exact device model and software version involved.
4. Separate confirmed observations from assumptions.
5. State licensing and redistribution considerations.
6. Remove personal identifiers and private device data from logs.

## Safety and scope

- Do not ask contributors to bypass security controls.
- Do not submit instructions that risk bricking a device without a safe, verified recovery plan.
- Do not include proprietary Apple firmware, system images, signing keys, credentials, or personal data.
- Do not claim iOS 27.2, Apple ID, App Store, or hardware features work without reproducible evidence.
- Respect all upstream licenses and preserve required notices.

## Reporting test results

Include the device model, firmware build, source revision, test setup, steps, result, logs (redacted), and known limitations. A test on another Snapdragon 732G phone is useful research but does not prove Moto G60 compatibility.
